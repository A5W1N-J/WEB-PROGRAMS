cofee

Coffee is a globally popular beverage brewed from the roasted and ground seeds—known as beans—of the Coffea plant. Originating from the highlands of Ethiopia and early traditions in Yemen, the drink is celebrated for its rich aroma, distinct bitter taste, and natural caffeine content that provides a stimulating energy boost. While the commercial market relies primarily on Arabica for its smooth flavor and Robusta for its bold strength, the journey from a harvested coffee cherry to a finished cup involves meticulous drying, roasting, and brewing styles like drip, French press, or espresso. Eaten or sipped across countless cultures, it remains a daily ritual and a cornerstone of social connection worldwide.
