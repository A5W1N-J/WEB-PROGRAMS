FESTIVALS IN INDIA

Indian festivals are a brilliant reflection of the country's diverse heritage, beautifully exemplified by Onam, Diwali, and Holi. In the southern state of Kerala, the 10-day harvest festival of Onam welcomes the legendary King Mahabali with intricate flower carpets, thrilling snake boat races, and the grand, multi-course Sadya feast served on banana leaves. As autumn arrives, the entire nation unites for Diwali, the spectacular Festival of Lights, where homes are illuminated with clay lamps, vibrant rangolis, and fireworks to celebrate the triumph of good over evil and welcome prosperity. Finally, the arrival of spring brings Holi, the joyous Festival of Colors, a high-energy celebration where people break down social barriers to splash water and vibrant powders, dancing to rhythmic drumbeats and sharing sweet delicacies. Together, these three celebrations capture the true essence of Indian culture through their shared spirit of unity, gratitude, and boundless joy.
