ONAM

Onam is the most vibrant and widely celebrated harvest and cultural festival of Kerala, India, uniting people across all communities in a grand ten-day celebration of peace, prosperity, and unity. The festival marks the homecoming of the legendary, benevolent Asura King Mahabali, whose mythical reign is remembered as a golden era of absolute equality and abundance. To welcome their beloved king, people adorn the entrances of their homes with intricate floral carpets known as Pookkalam, dress in traditional off-white attire, and prepare the world-famous Onasadya—a majestic multi-course vegetarian feast served on banana leaves. The festive spirit reverberates across the state through spectacular cultural spectacles, including the thrilling Vallam Kali (snake boat races), the energetic Pulikali (tiger dances), and elegant folk performances. Beyond its mythological roots, Onam is a profound reflection of Kerala's rich cultural heritage, showcasing a beautiful secular spirit where traditions are joyfully passed down through generations.
