phone

A smartphone is a compact, handheld device that combines traditional mobile calling with the power of a portable computer. Featuring a touch-sensitive screen, it lets users browse the internet, send emails, capture high-resolution photos, and navigate via GPS. Beyond basic texting and voice calls, smartphones support thousands of apps for social media, mobile banking, work productivity, and entertainment. While these devices have transformed daily life by making communication and information instantly accessible, overuse can lead to distractions, reduced face-to-face interaction, and health concerns.
