ABOUT US

The College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) is a prominent public technical institution in Kerala, India, established in 1993 by the Government of Kerala under the management of the Institute of Human Resources Development (IHRD). Located in the Alappuzha district, the college is approved by the AICTE and affiliated with the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). It is highly regarded for its undergraduate B.Tech programs, postgraduate MCA course, and a highly active student ecosystem that features one of the region's most decorated IEEE Student Branches. Over more than three decades, CEC has built a strong reputation for academic excellence, technical innovation, and consistently high campus placement rates with top-tier global IT and engineering firms.
